About DL Equipment & Aggregates

Built in Saint Lucia. Ready for serious work.

DL Equipment & Aggregates Ltd is a Saint Lucian construction and heavy-equipment company based at Hill 20, Babonneau. The company brings construction, demolition, land development, equipment rental, haulage and material supply together under one operation.

What began with one dump truck has grown into the people, machinery and supporting services needed to take on demanding work across the island.

The company story

A business built one capability at a time.

DL began in 2014 with a single dump truck and a clear belief that there was room in Saint Lucia for a dependable, technically capable operation that understood the work from the ground up.

The company was founded by Denis Louis, originally from Cacao, Babonneau, and built alongside his wife, Lindy Louis, who has been part of the journey from day one. Denis's early career in auto mechanics, heavy-equipment repair, machinery operation and transport gave the young company a practical foundation. DL did not begin by simply owning equipment; it began with an understanding of how that equipment works, how it should be maintained and what it takes to keep a job moving.

As demand grew, the company reinvested. One truck led to another, and the operation steadily developed beyond trucking. In 2017, DL expanded into heavy equipment and the sale of aggregates and construction materials. This was an important step in shaping the company as it exists today: a business able to combine machinery, transport, materials and field execution instead of treating each as a separate service.

That steady expansion has given DL the depth to work in different ways. The company can undertake construction, demolition and land-development projects directly, or work alongside contractors by supplying the equipment, operators, haulage and materials their projects require.

Today, DL operates with a core team of approximately 28 people, expanding its workforce when larger projects require it. The company continues to grow with the same practical approach that shaped its beginning: understand the work, assemble what is needed and follow through on delivery.

DL today

More than an equipment fleet.

DL has grown to support a project from early site work through transport, material supply and construction. That range is especially valuable on large jobs, where fewer hand-offs can mean clearer coordination and a more responsive programme.

Construction, demolition and development

The team carries out construction, major demolition, excavation, site clearing and preparation, road development and wider land-development work. DL can take raw land and establish the access, levels and working platform required to move a project forward.

Equipment and transport

The rental fleet includes seven excavators ranging from approximately 3.5 to 35 tonnes, plus a bulldozer, one grader, rollers, a backhoe, dumpers, forklifts and crane trucks. DL operates 20-tonne dump trucks, smaller trucks and trailer trucks with lowboys, flatbeds and 20- and 40-foot beds. Heavy equipment haulage is available for loads up to 30 tonnes, subject to the route and site access.

Materials and production

DL makes its own concrete blocks and supplies sand, gravel, crushed stone, cement and ready-mix concrete from Hill 20. Customers can arrange collection with the depot or discuss supply and transport for a larger project.

Supported in-house

An in-house garage helps DL maintain and repair its own fleet. Mechanics, electricians, welders and body technicians give the company direct technical support and help keep trucks and machinery ready for the field. It is a level of self-sufficiency that has become central to how the operation works.

Looking forward

Growing towards the next major project.

DL's ambition is to become a stronger Saint Lucian partner for large contractors, developers and major projects—without losing the practical, hands-on approach that built the company.

The business is continuing to deepen its concrete and crane capabilities and exploring ways to expand its aggregate supply. That growth is not simply about owning more machinery. It is about building the capacity to respond when a project is large, complex or time-sensitive.

DL has already demonstrated that capability through road works, wall construction and the demolition of substantial buildings, including hotel projects. The company now wants more of Saint Lucia to understand the true scale of the operation and what its team can deliver.

Whether DL is leading the work or supporting another contractor, the objective remains the same: bring together the people, equipment, transport and materials needed to move the project from plan to completion.

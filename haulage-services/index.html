Haulage & trucking services across Saint Lucia

DL handles one-off moves and ongoing site transport across Saint Lucia, with trailer trucks, lowboys, flatbeds, 20- and 40-foot beds, and dump trucks.

Heavy equipment haulage

Haul excavators, bulldozers and other plant between yards and job sites. Confirm the machine’s weight and dimensions so DL can assess the trailer, route and site access.

Container haulage & freight

Arrange collection and delivery of containers and other freight. Share the load details, collection point and delivery access to plan the move.

Construction material delivery

Move sand, gravel, crushed stone and other construction materials to site. DL can coordinate transport with materials ordered from the Hill 20 depot.

General trucking

Arrange trucking for construction work, including smaller loads and construction debris from demolition and site clearance.

Plan the route and site access

DL can haul heavy equipment up to 30 tonnes, depending on the load, route and access. We also operate 20-tonne dump trucks for bulk materials. For machinery and containers, we check the dimensions, how the load will be handled, and whether the truck can reach both sites.

If the move includes a lift or equipment hire, share that when you enquire so DL can check the right equipment and timing together.

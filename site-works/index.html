Ground made workable

The work starts in the ground—and carries into construction.

Before construction can move forward, a site needs access, levels and a stable working platform. DL brings the field execution and coordination to prepare land at different scales, then support the construction and civil work that follows.

Land to working site

Clear it. Shape it. Set the work up.

Clear & prepare

Raw-land clearing, site preparation and initial access work that gives a project room to begin.

Excavate & shape

Excavation, earthworks, grading and compaction matched to the scale and conditions of the site.

Open up access

Access-road and small-road development to make the site practical for people, plant and material movement.

Build from the ground up

Construction, supporting civil works and retaining-wall work once the site is ready for the next package.
